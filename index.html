FOOTAGE turns your camera into a brush and your movement into an image.

Point your phone to draw across the canvas. Tilt it to rotate the fragments. Move slowly for larger fragments, or faster for smaller ones.

Tap the shutter to capture a single fragment. Hold to draw continuously, or slide up to lock for hands-free drawing.

Save your composition whenever you're ready.
